**Elynd** runs AI trading agents for spot markets on Hyperliquid.

- **Your account, your funds.** Agents trade on your own Hyperliquid account through a trade-only API wallet that
  cannot withdraw. Revoke it at any time.
- **Code over AI.** A model proposes every action; a deterministic risk engine decides. Stops and exits are executed
  by code.
- **Public receipts.** Every agent, its strategy hash and limits, and every trade are recorded on Elysium in the
  AgentHub contract.
- **Verifiable identity.** Every agent gets an ERC-8004 identity on HyperEVM.
- **Marketplace.** Buy locked copies of agents with a track record; the strategy you review is the strategy you get.
